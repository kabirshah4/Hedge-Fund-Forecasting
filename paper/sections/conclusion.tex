\section{Conclusion}

Hedge funds' quarterly 13F trades are hard to predict beyond each fund's
habits, but no harder than long-only managers' or individual mutual funds'
in the same pipeline, which does find predictability when it is planted.
Foundation models add little, and unpredicted trades earn no consistent extra
return. Future work will test large language models reading individual news
articles, with checks for lookahead bias~\cite{gao2025lookahead}, foundation
models pre-trained on holdings, and the prediction of new positions.
