\section{Conclusion}

Hedge funds' quarterly 13F trades are hard to predict beyond each fund's
habits. However, they are no harder to predict than the trades of long-only
managers or individual mutual funds in the same pipeline, and the pipeline
does find predictability when we plant it. Foundation models and an LLM's
reading of news add little, and unpredicted trades earn no consistent extra
return. Future work could pre-train foundation models on holdings, have LLMs
read full articles, and predict new positions.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.} All data are free and public (SEC 13F and Form
ADV, OpenFIGI, Yahoo Finance, FNSPID, policyuncertainty.com, Kenneth French's
data library). The code, fund lists and every result table are in an anonymized
repository: \url{https://anonymous.4open.science/r/hedge-fund-13f}.
