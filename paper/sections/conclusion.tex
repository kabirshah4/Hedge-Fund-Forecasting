\section{Conclusion}

Hedge funds' quarterly 13F trades are hard to predict beyond each fund's
habits. However, they are no harder to predict than the trades of long-only
managers or individual mutual funds in the same pipeline, and the pipeline
does find predictability when we plant it. Foundation models add little, and
unpredicted trades earn no consistent extra return. In future work, we aim to
test large language models that read individual news articles, with checks for
lookahead bias~\cite{gao2025lookahead}, foundation models pre-trained on
holdings, and the prediction of new positions.
